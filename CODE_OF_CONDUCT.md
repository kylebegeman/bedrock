# Code Of Conduct

Bedrock should be a practical, respectful, and technically serious project.
This code applies to project spaces including issues, discussions, pull
requests, reviews, documentation, and community chat.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct, factual, and constructive.
- Assume mistakes can be fixed, but do not ignore risk.
- Respect different experience levels while keeping the technical bar clear.
- Keep discussions focused on the work, user impact, and maintainability.
- Report security issues through private channels instead of public threads.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- harassment, threats, insults, or personal attacks
- discriminatory language or conduct
- publishing private information, credentials, production data, or personal
  contact details without permission
- sustained disruption of technical discussions
- knowingly submitting malicious code, unsafe instructions, or secret material

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments; close issues or pull requests;
temporarily limit participation; or ban contributors who violate this code.
Enforcement should be proportionate, consistent, and focused on protecting the
project and its users.

To report a conduct concern, contact the maintainers through the repository's
private maintainer channels. Do not include security exploit details in conduct
reports unless the maintainer asks for them through a private vulnerability
reporting channel.
